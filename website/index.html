GET TO YOUR MUSIC

Three steps. One setlist.

Connect your library

Choose the folder or drive containing your music. Select VirtualDJ or Serato, then confirm the detected playlist destination or change it.

Add your requests

Type or speak a playlist idea, paste a song list, or upload a photo. Choose your library only, or include suggestions to find later.

Review and export

Listen to possible matches and approve your songs. Setlist creates the playlist; you stay in control of the final selection.

SPOTIFY PLAYLIST INTEGRATION

Turn your Spotify playlist
into a DJ setlist.

Paste a Spotify playlist link and connect your Spotify account. Setlist reads the song titles and artists, keeps their order, and matches them to music in your library. Review the matches, see which songs you still need, then export your playlist.

Connect your own Spotify account. During beta, Spotify access is limited to invited testers. Import playlists you own or collaborate on. You can also paste a song list or upload a screenshot without connecting Spotify.

Setlist imports playlist information. It does not download audio from Spotify.

A few things to know

Do I need to organize my library first?[+]

Setlist uses labels and filenames to look for songs, including possible matches for imperfect names. Review uncertain matches before exporting.

Does it download songs?[+]

No. Setlist matches your existing files. Missing songs remain identifiable so you can obtain them separately; they are not playable audio.

What about Serato and Spotify?[+]

Serato export is experimental. It writes legacy .crate files, needs songs on the destination drive, and has not been verified in a real Serato installation, especially version 4+. Test a small playlist first. Missing songs are saved in a separate text list.

Spotify sign-in is currently limited to approved testers. Everyone can use pasted song lists and photos without a Spotify connection.

What does setup require?[+]

This is a Windows desktop app. Python is included; the desktop window uses Microsoft WebView2. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows may display an unrecognized-publisher notice. Download only from the official link on this page.
